Tiny5 Duo is the bold companion to Tiny5, a compact 5-pixel variable font that captures the essence of 1980s–90s digital minimalism. Where Tiny5 draws every stroke one pixel wide, Tiny5 Duo doubles its vertical stems, giving the same essential letterforms a sturdier, more emphatic presence—ideal for headlines, labels and anywhere a little extra weight helps the text hold its own.

Like Tiny5, it features six variable axes — Weight, Width, Italic, Roundness, Bleed and Jitter — giving you precise control over its look: from crisp geometric shapes and sharp LCD edges to the soft glow of CRT monitors and the subtle ink spread of dot-matrix printers. Set alongside Tiny5, it provides a natural bold counterpart that stays true to the pixel-perfect aesthetic. The family also has a monospaced companion, Tiny5 Mono, which sets the same letterforms in a fixed cell for code editors, terminals and text-mode interfaces.

Tiny5 Duo excels at evoking retro-futurism, constrained-tech nostalgia, and clean minimalism. It's especially well-suited for:

  • Titles, headings and HUDs in pixel art and lo-fi games
  • Emphasis in terminal-style interfaces and embedded systems
  • Labels and buttons in UI/UX design
  • Branding with a distinct 8/16-bit or vintage electronics vibe

Tiny5 Duo shares the broad language support of Tiny5, covering Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and Armenian scripts across 974 languages. Like Tiny5, it goes well beyond them with IPA, mathematical operators, arrows, geometric shapes, dingbats, Braille, block elements and the sextants and much of Symbols for Legacy Computing, for a total of 4,094 glyphs. It also has small caps for Latin and Cyrillic.

For pixel-perfect results, set the font size to increments of 6 pt (8 px).

All three fonts are also available in BDF format for seamless integration with the mcu-renderer, u8g2 and TFT_eSPI libraries.
